Our Mission: Quenching The World With Kindness

It’s easy to take reliable access to clean, disease-free water for granted. Easier still to feel disenfranchised in a world rife with suffering and inequality. Nevertheless, we seek to revitalize those afflicted by water insecurity and to uplift like-minded individuals who yearn for a brighter future.

We would like to present you with an opportunity to vicariously channel your convictions into a movement that delivers impoverished communities from needless hardship. Even the smallest contributions move mountains, and we have proof:

Since 2003, Charity: Water has funded more than 100,000 initiatives that have brought drinkable water to tens of millions of people in nearly 30 countries across the globe.

Human empathy is more than just philosophy; it’s a force of nature, unparalleled in its capacity to heal and unify. This core belief is what drives our continued efforts in our fight against the Global Water Crisis.

Together, we are architects of the future. Join the cause, and become a steward of humanity.
